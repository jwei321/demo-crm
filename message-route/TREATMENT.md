# There and Back — treatment

7.0 s · 1920×1080 · 24 fps · Isometric Infographic · no narration, no subtitles (the stage labels carry the film) · English type

## Brief

How a message travels from your phone to a data centre and back. Labelled stages
phone → cell tower → fibre-optic cable → data centre; a glowing packet travels the route;
each label appears as the packet reaches it. Score and foley synthesized in code.

## Three candidate structures

1. **One journey, out and back (chosen).** A single tracked packet leaves the phone, the camera follows it
   station by station, it changes colour inside the data centre (request → reply) and races home while the
   camera pulls out to the finished infographic. Fits "there and back" literally, and 7 s holds one route twice.
2. **The finished infographic comes apart.** Open on the whole labelled board, then dive to each station.
   Rejected: the labels would already be on screen, which breaks "each label appears as the packet reaches it".
3. **Two boards, here vs there.** Phone board and data-centre board, linked by a cable crossing the cut.
   Rejected: a board switch costs a second we don't have, and hides the cable, the most surprising stage.

## Logline and arc

A tap sends one amber packet through the air, down a mast and along a buried glass thread to a humming
building; it comes back mint, twice as fast, and the whole route is suddenly in view.

- **Setup** (0–1.5 s): close on an oversized phone; tap; the packet is born on the screen and jumps to the tower.
- **Turn** (1.5–4.5 s): the ground is cut open to show the fibre; the packet streaks to the data centre,
  which lights up rack by rack and turns it into a reply. A near-silence before it leaves.
- **Ending** (4.5–7 s): the reply rushes home along the same line while the camera pulls back; it lands on the
  phone screen where the film began (bookend), with every station labelled: a finished infographic.

## Benchmark

- *Story of Send* (Google's 2012 explainer of an email's route): learn the strict station order, one idea per
  station, the route line as the spine of the frame. Don't take its illustrations, icons, colours or copy.
- *Powers of Ten*: learn the continuous log zoom of the final pull-back. Don't take any shot or framing.

## Board and colour

One floating diorama board (15 × 7 units, 1.4 deep, strata: turf, soil, bedrock) on an off-white ground
with an iso dot lattice. Not to scale: the phone is a monument.

| Hue | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Amber `#F2A33A` | the request packet (reserved: nothing else is amber) |
| Mint `#3FC8A0` | the reply packet (reserved) |
| Electric blue `#2F6BFF` | signal paths: radio arcs, the fibre core, screen glow |
| Graphite `#4A4E5C` | infrastructure: tower, data-centre shell |
| Coral `#E8735A` | the person's world: the phone body |
| Sage `#9DB98A` / umber `#9A7656` / slate `#7C8291` | turf / soil / bedrock |

Ink `#231A2C`. Ground `#F3F0E8`.

| t | Picture | Sound |
|---|---|---|
| 0.00 | phone close, screen dark | digital silence |

| 0.20–0.50 | PHONE pin draws | — |
| 0.50 | packet launches; dashed radio arc drawn just ahead | whoosh up, pulse starts (eighths) |
| 1.50 | packet hits the antenna; blue rings | radio chirp + marimba note 2; CELL TOWER pin |
| 1.50–2.25 | packet runs down the mast into the ground | descending tick run |
| 2.00–2.30 | dashed cut line draws along the board front | scribe |
| 2.30–2.65 | front skin slides out, hatched rim fades in | soil slide (low noise) |
| 2.50 | packet enters the fibre core; FIBRE-OPTIC CABLE pin | glass zing + marimba note 3 |
| 2.50–3.90 | packet streaks along the core | high shimmer bed |
| 3.50 | — | J-cut: machine-room hum fades in |

| 4.25–4.50 | everything holds | near-silence: only the hum |
| 4.50 | packet leaves as mint reply; REQUEST / REPLY legend | downbeat: glock + bass, pulse returns at double time |
| 4.50–5.90 | reply races home, camera pulls out | notes 4-3-2-1 descending, one per station passed |
| 6.00 | reply lands on the phone; screen turns mint, check mark | ping + resolving chord |
| 6.00–7.00 | locked wide, finished infographic | chord rings out, city air |

## Cue map

120 bpm, 4/4, 3½ bars. Bar 1 (0–2 s): pulse on eighths, marimba on stations. Bar 2 (2–4 s): add woodblock
on quarters, shimmer under the fibre. Beat 8.5 (4.25 s): drop-out. Bar 3 (4.5–6.5 s): double-time pulse,
glock answers, bass on 1 and 3. 6.0 s: final chord on the downbeat of beat 12. Every station hit lands on a beat
(0.15 tap is the only off-grid sound, a pickup).

## Sound design

| Section | Ambience | Foley | Music |
|---|---|---|---|
| Phone (0–1.5) | light outdoor air | tap, whoosh | marimba pickup, pulse in |
| Tower (1.5–2.5) | air + faint wind | radio chirp, tick run down the mast | pulse + marimba |
| Fibre (2.5–4) | air ducks; shimmer bed | cut scribe, soil slide, glass zing | + woodblock |
| Data centre (3.5–4.5) | machine-room hum (J-cut in at 3.5) | LED servo clicks | drops out at 4.25 |
| Return (4.5–7) | hum fades, air returns | whooshes per station, phone ping | glock + bass, final chord |

Silences: digital zero 0–0.15 s; near-silence 4.25–4.5 s. Sound transitions: J-cut hum (3.5), L-cut of the
chord over the locked end. Master −14 LUFS.

## Type

Jost (OFL). Stage labels 600 caps, tracking 0.12 em, 30 px, with a 7 px ground-colour halo, on pins
(dot → leader → rule → text): PHONE up-left, CELL TOWER up, FIBRE-OPTIC CABLE down (below the board),
DATA CENTRE up-right. Each pin draws in ~0.3 s from the moment the packet arrives and stays to the end.
Title top-left for the whole film: A MESSAGE, THERE AND BACK. Legend bottom-left from 4.5 s: amber cube
REQUEST, mint cube REPLY, NOT TO SCALE.
